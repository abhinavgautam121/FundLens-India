# Product demonstration — approximately 4 minutes

**0:00–0:35 — Problem and overview.** “High returns alone do not explain consistency, risk or
data reliability. FundLens India turns NAV history into transparent research evidence.”
Point out the DEMO DATA banner, 24 fictional schemes, three synthetic benchmarks and withheld
scores. State the independent educational purpose and absence of investment recommendations.

**0:35–1:10 — Fund Explorer.** Filter to Equity, Active. Show return, volatility, drawdown,
coverage and peer context together. Change historical period and export CSV. Explain that
missing values stay unavailable.

**1:10–2:00 — Deep Dive and benchmark.** Select DEMO Cedar Equity Research Fund. Open its
normalised growth chart. Explain common start value and verified TRI comparison. Open Rolling
returns and then Risk & drawdown. Point to worst/median rolling outcomes, negative peak loss,
and the configurable risk-free assumption.

**2:00–3:05 — Explainability Lab.** Show four component percentiles and weighted contributions.
State that weights are illustrative and peer-relative. Compare 30 Oct 2025 and 30 Oct 2026.
Use actual displayed metric differences and the waterfall; do not script a claim about a
particular increase/decrease in advance. Explain that peer changes can affect percentiles
and the software does not infer causal market explanations.

**3:05–3:35 — Data Quality Centre.** Show the deliberately short-history and missing-observation
alerts. Switch to Uploaded data and show manual AMFI, benchmark and metadata imports. Explain
duplicate/conflict handling and TRI validation. Show Power BI ZIP and audit exports.

**3:35–4:00 — Value and limitations.** “A reusable analytics pipeline, traceable imports,
tested calculations and visible assumptions make research easier to inspect.” Mention weekday
calendar approximation, small demo cohorts, current-only metadata and the need for stronger
data reconciliation before production. End on the product view, with its disclaimer visible.
